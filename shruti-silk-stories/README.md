# Shruti Silk Stories — Luxury Handloom Saree Website

A recreation of the luxury Indian handloom silk saree e-commerce website featured in the video showcase.

## ✨ Implemented Sections & Features

1. **Top Announcement Bar**:
   - Deep royal maroon/burgundy banner with festive badge (`UTSAV 20% OFF`), discount code (`UTSAV20`), and free insured shipping tracker across India.
   - Interactive atmospheric loom/tanpura sound generator (Web Audio API).

2. **Luxury Glassmorphism Navigation**:
   - Sticky blur header with regal typography, live search modal, wishlist indicator, and shopping bag slide-over drawer.

3. **"More Than a Saree" Hero Section**:
   - Seamless looping ambient video of the boutique showroom extracted directly from the demonstration video (`assets/hero_showroom_video.mp4`).
   - "Six weavers, one thread — months of patient hands" headline and story typography.
   - 100% Silk Mark certified trust ribbons.

4. **"SIX MOODS." Curated Arch Gallery**:
   - Exact 6-arch layout from the video (Royal Peacock Teal, Sunlit Haldi, Sindoor Rust, Rani Gulabi, Imperial Wine, Forest Emerald).
   - Interactive hover zoom, price tags, and Quick View triggers.

5. **"Woven to Be Remembered" 3D Curved Cylindrical Carousel**:
   - True 3D perspective cylindrical card slider with dragging/touch swipe, prev/next arrows, and active indicators.

6. **"The Bridal Collection" - Dark Organic Wave Curvature Section**:
   - Organic curved SVG wave dividers, rich obsidian background, featured crimson bridal showcase card, and horizontal scrolling product catalogue.

7. **"Autumn — Winter '26" & "IVORY ORGANZA BLOOM" Interactive Product Detail View**:
   - Multi-angle thumbnail column, high-res model view, interactive color swatch switcher (Warm Ivory, Blush Rose, Royal Crimson), blouse customization buttons, and the signature curved maroon `ADD TO CART` pill button.

8. **"First Light at the Loom"**:
   - Cinematic chapter switcher exploring generational weaving families in Kanchipuram and Varanasi.

9. **"LATEST TRENDS" Editorial Magazine**:
   - Modern saree draping masterclasses and heirloom preservation guides.

10. **Slide-over E-Commerce Engine**:
    - Interactive bag drawer with quantity counters, coupon code applicator (`UTSAV20`), dynamic subtotal/discount calculations, free shipping threshold progress bar, and express checkout modal.
